# Tecost SA

> Tecost SA is a Swiss health-IT company, founded in 1997 and headquartered in
> Fribourg, that designs, builds, implements and operates clinical information
> systems for hospitals, clinics, long-term care institutions and home-care
> organizations. Its product line is the Carefolio suite, and it is a named
> industry partner of the openEHR Foundation.

## What an agent should know first

Tecost SA has **no public API**. As of 2026-09-02 the company publishes no
developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/Swagger/GraphQL/AsyncAPI
document, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no SDK in any public package
registry, and no /.well-known/ document of any kind. Carefolio is delivered as a
per-customer deployment: tenant hosts such as daler.carefolio.ch redirect to
Microsoft Entra ID SAML sign-in, and the shared Carefolio host answers HTTP 403
to anonymous requests. Any integration contract Tecost ships reaches customers
and their integrators under a commercial agreement, not through a public
surface.

If you are trying to integrate with a Carefolio installation, the entry point is
the institution that operates it, or Tecost directly at office@tecost.ch —
not a self-service developer signup, which does not exist.

## Products (Carefolio suite)

- [Carefolio Acute](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-acute): acute-care hospital information system.
- [Carefolio Rehabilitation](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-rehabilitation): rehabilitation clinics.
- [Carefolio Psy](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-psy): psychiatric care.
- [Carefolio LongTerm](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-longterm): geriatric, medico-social and socio-educational institutions.
- [Carefolio AtHome](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-athome): home-care organizations.
- [Carefolio Critical Care 3C](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-critical-care-3c): intensive care units.
- [Carefolio Network](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-network): patient coordination across care networks.
- [Carefolio Portal](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-portal): provider-to-patient interaction platform.
- [Carefolio DIH](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-ehr): Data Integration Hub, described by Tecost as openEHR-based, for exchanging structured and unstructured data between providers and the Swiss electronic patient record.
- [Carefolio AI](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-ai-artificial-intelligence): AI tooling across the suite.
- [Carefolio Workshop](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/solutions/carefolio-workshop): configuration and customization environment.

## Services

- [Services overview](https://www.tecost.ch/fr/services): process and organizational consulting, solution implementation and change management, custom software development, 24/7 maintenance and support, SaaS hosting and security.

## Interoperability

- [openEHR Foundation industry partners](https://openehr.org/industry-partners/): Tecost SA is listed, stating that it is "making [openEHR] the cornerstone of the sustainability and interoperability of clinical data".
- Tecost product material also names HL7 and SOAP web services as third-party integration mechanisms. No conformance profile, message-type list, WSDL or openEHR artifact is published for any of these.
